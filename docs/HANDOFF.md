# Product Owner Handoff

Phase 2 adds a complete side-by-side car ownership comparison at `/car-ownership-cost-calculator` and an original guide at `/guides/total-car-ownership-cost-pakistan`.

The calculator compares depreciation, fuel, maintenance, insurance, taxes/fees, and one-time costs over three or five years. It includes validated illustrative inputs, reset behavior, cost cards, a responsive composition chart, a detailed table, monthly/per-kilometre results, and a neutral lower-cost highlight. It explicitly remains a simplified cash-purchase model.

Automated checks and production verification are documented in `docs/TEST_REPORT.md`. SEO route/canonical details are in `docs/SEO_AUDIT.md`; formulas and constraints are in `docs/CALCULATION_METHODS.md`.

Product decisions for a later phase: whether to add a separately designed financing model, whether to add a verified data source, and whether to introduce browser-level regression testing. No Phase 3 work has begun.
